## Source Hierarchy (Critical Rule)

| Priority | Source | Use in Final Text |
|----------|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Ra Contact Vol 1-2** | Paraphrased (never quoted directly) |
| 2 | **Q'uo Archives Vol 9-11** | NEVER appears in final text |
| 3 | **Gospels** | Natural parallels and echoes — NEVER with chapter:verse citations |
| 4 | **Wisdom traditions** | As enrichment (early Christianity, comparative religion) |
| 5 | **Historical studies** | When naturally relevant |

**Golden Rule**: Q'uo helps you understand Ra. Ra is the book's only voice. Gospels provide natural parallels but are never cited with chapter:verse — the resonance must feel organic, not academic.

## Gospel Usage Rules

- Echo phrases and themes from the Gospels as natural parallels
- Show convergence between Jesus's teachings and the Ra perspective
- NEVER cite chapter:verse (e.g., never write "John 3:16" or "Matthew 5:3")
- NEVER quote Scripture directly — paraphrase or echo naturally
- The reader should feel the connection without needing a Bible reference
